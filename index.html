About

I design for the moments that matter most in people's work.

I'm a UX architect with deep roots in healthcare technology. Over the years I've designed and shipped products used by thousands of providers and clinical staff — work that required balancing clinical safety, regulatory compliance, and the very human need for tools that don't get in your way.

I've led research operations, built design systems, authored UX career frameworks, and more recently embedded AI into how my team synthesizes research and generates production-ready components. I care about the full craft: the system and the pixel, the research and the release.

I'm currently exploring new opportunities where I can bring both deep IC craft and cross-functional leadership to teams working on meaningful problems.
